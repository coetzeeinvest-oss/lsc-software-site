Features

Everything the school run needs.

Not a generic fleet tool relabelled — every screen below is built around learners, parents, drivers and the schools they travel to.

Scheduling & dispatch

Recurring weekly routes, to-school and from-school, with driver assignment and status tracking from scheduled through to dropped off.

Live tracking

Parents see the vehicle move in real time; drivers log pickups, drop-offs and exceptions from the road.

Parent portal

Upcoming trips, live status per child, and a weekly schedule view — no phone calls to the office.

Invoicing & billing

Distance-based pricing tiers per school, VAT-ready client invoices, generated straight from completed trips.

Your own branding

Logo, colour palette and fonts on your subdomain — parents see your business, not ours.

Notifications

Schedule changes, delays and confirmations reach parents and drivers automatically.
