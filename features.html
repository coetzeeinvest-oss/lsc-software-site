Features

Everything the school run needs.

Not a generic fleet tool relabelled. Every screen below, from the LiftMe driver app to the parent portal, is built around learners, parents, drivers and the school transport routes they run every day.

Scheduling & dispatch

Recurring weekly routes, to-school and from-school, with driver assignment and status tracking from scheduled through to dropped off.

Live tracking

Parents see the vehicle move in real time; drivers log pickups, drop-offs and exceptions from the road in the LiftMe app.

Parent portal

Upcoming trips, live status per child, and a weekly schedule view. No phone calls to the office.

Invoicing & billing

Distance-based pricing tiers per school, VAT-ready client invoices, generated straight from completed trips. Only need a quick invoice? Try the free school transport invoice template.

Your own branding

Logo, colour palette and fonts on your subdomain. Parents see your business, not ours.

Notifications

Schedule changes, delays and confirmations reach parents and drivers automatically.
