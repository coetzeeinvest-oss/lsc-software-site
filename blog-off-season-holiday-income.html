Turn the December holidays into income: Off Season mode for school transport operators

Operators · 2 min read
Back to blog

The school run stops in December, but your vehicle's costs do not. Finance, insurance and servicing carry on while the morning and afternoon runs go quiet until schools reopen in January. Meanwhile, the parents who trust you with their children every day are looking for lifts to birthday parties, holiday camps, sports fixtures and the airport.

That demand is real, and you are the obvious person to meet it. Off Season mode in LiftMe lets you offer it properly, using the same portal, drivers and vehicles your families already know.

Your holidays, put to work

Off Season mode turns your parent portal into a simple booking service for one-off trips. Parents ask for a lift, you decide which requests to take and who drives, and everyone stays informed from the booking to the drop-off. No more requests buried in WhatsApp chats or a notebook.

Why it suits school transport operators

  • Your parents already trust you. Your portal is invite-only: you create the parent accounts, so the people booking are families you already know, not strangers from a public app.
  • Parents know who is collecting their child. The lift is run by one of your own drivers in one of your own vehicles. That is the peace of mind parents are really buying for an evening lift home from a party.
  • Extra income from what you already own. The vehicle, the drivers and the parent relationships are all in place. This is a way to earn from them in the weeks when the school run is quiet.
  • Everything in one place. Holiday trips sit alongside your school routes in the same system, in line with the operations discipline that keeps a transport business calm.
  • You stay in charge. You choose what to accept, so holiday work never gets in the way of your school commitments.

A simple plan for this December

  1. Think about who will ask. Which families have already asked you for a Saturday lift or a trip to the airport? Those are your first Off Season customers.
  2. Decide how much holiday work you want. Perhaps weekend events only, or full days for a holiday programme. Off Season mode follows your lead.
  3. Tell your parents. A short message that they can now book event lifts through the portal they already use is enough to get started.
