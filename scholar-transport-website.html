What your website includes

Everything a family looks for before they call you

New families want to know who you are, which schools you drive to and what it costs. Your LiftMe website answers all three.

Your brand, front and centre

Your logo, colours and business name on your own web address, so families see your business and not ours.

A landing page for your business

A headline, photos, trust points and a short how-it-works, so a new family can tell what you offer in seconds.

The schools you serve

List the schools you drive to, so a parent can see straight away that you cover theirs.

Per-trip prices

Show your price tiers on the page instead of sending a price list as a photo in a chat.

A get-a-quote calculator

A family enters their home address, picks their school and sees an estimated price per trip, based on the driving distance.

A parent login

Parents sign in on the same address to see trips and live tracking. It also works as a school transport app on their phone.

More than a brochure site

The website is connected to the software that runs your business

A normal scholar transport website advertises you and then leaves you to answer the same questions by message: what does it cost, do you go to this school, where is the vehicle right now.

Your LiftMe website answers the first two before a family has contacted you, and the third once they have joined. The same address is where parents log in for trips and live tracking, and where you run routes, drivers and invoices, so there is one link to share and one place to keep up to date. Need to send an invoice before you are set up? The free scholar transport invoice template makes one in a few minutes.
