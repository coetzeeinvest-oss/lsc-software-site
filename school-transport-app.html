Why LiftMe is a web app, and why that suits the school run

Most people expect a school transport app to mean searching the App Store or Google Play, waiting for a download and hoping their phone is supported. LiftMe works differently. It opens from a link in your phone's browser and looks and feels like an app, so there is nothing to find, nothing to download and nothing to keep up to date.

That matters on the school run. A new parent can be using it within a minute of getting the link. A driver who changes phones just signs in again. And when we improve something, there is no app update for anyone to install.

It is also your business's app, not a generic one. Parents see your company's name, logo and colours, because every operator runs LiftMe on their own branded web address.

Add the school transport app to your home screen

You can use LiftMe from the browser, or put it next to your other apps so it opens in one tap.

1

Open your link

Open your transport company's LiftMe link in your phone's browser and sign in.

2

Add it to your home screen

On iPhone, tap the Share button in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the menu in Chrome and tap Add to Home screen or Install app.

3

Open it like any other app

It opens without the browser bar, with your operator's name and icon, next to the apps you already use.
