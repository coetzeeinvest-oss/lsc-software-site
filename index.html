Why LiftMe exists

The school run, run properly. Every operator, every day.

Most transport operators started with a WhatsApp group, a paper route list, and a driver's own cellphone number handed out to worried parents. That works until you're running six vehicles across three schools and a parent calls the office asking where their child is right now.

LiftMe replaces the group chat and the spreadsheet with one system built specifically for the school run: routes that repeat automatically every week without re-entering them, a driver app that logs each pickup and drop-off from the road as it happens, and a parent portal that answers "where's my child" before anyone has to pick up the phone. Billing follows the same trip data automatically, so invoices match what actually happened on the road instead of what someone remembered to write down.

The result isn't just tidier software. It's a school run that runs properly, every single day, whether you operate one bakkie or a full depot across multiple schools.
