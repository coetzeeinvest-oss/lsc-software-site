01 Why LiftMe exists

Scholar transport isn't a smaller version of logistics.

It's a promise to a parent that their child got to school and back safely, every single day, on time, with someone accountable for every leg of the trip.

Generic fleet software tracks vehicles. LiftMe tracks a child's journey, and gives the parent, the driver and the office the same picture of it.

02 What we build around

A few things we don't compromise on.

Built for one job

Every screen exists because a driver, parent or office needed it for the school run specifically, not repurposed from a generic delivery app.

No lock-in

Month-to-month billing, cancel any time. We'd rather earn your subscription every month than trap you in a contract.

South African, by default

Transparent pricing, VAT-ready invoicing, and support in your timezone, built around how operators here actually run their business.

Your brand, not ours

Parents and schools see your logo, your colours, your subdomain. LiftMe is the engine underneath, and we're happy to stay out of sight.

04 How it works

From first email to live routes.

Step 1

Tell us about your fleet

Number of vehicles, schools you serve, and the branding you want parents to see. A quick email is enough to start.

Step 2

We set up your subdomain

Routes, drivers, pricing tiers and your branding get configured for you, with no setup fee.

Step 3

You go live, we stay on call

Parents get the portal link, drivers get the app, and billing runs month to month on whichever plan fits your fleet.
