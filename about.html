Why LiftMe exists

Scholar transport isn't a smaller version of logistics

It's a promise to a parent that their child got to school and back safely — every single day, on time, with someone accountable for every leg of the trip. Generic fleet software tracks vehicles. LiftMe tracks a child's journey, and gives the parent, the driver and the office the same picture of it.

What we build around

A few things we don't compromise on

Built for one job

Every screen exists because a driver, parent or office needed it for the school run specifically — not repurposed from a generic delivery app.

No lock-in

Month-to-month billing, cancel any time. We'd rather earn your subscription every month than trap you in a contract.

South African, by default

Transparent pricing, VAT-ready invoicing, and support in your timezone — built around how operators here actually run their business.

Your brand, not ours

Parents and schools see your logo, your colours, your subdomain. LiftMe is the engine underneath — we're happy to stay out of sight.
