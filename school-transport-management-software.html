Built for the school run

School transport management software vs generic fleet tracking

Generic fleet tools are built for couriers and logistics dispatch. They can show you where a vehicle is, but they know nothing about learners, schools or parents.

School transport management software starts from the school run itself. Routes repeat every week, every child has a trip status, every school has its own prices, and parents need to know the vehicle is on its way. That is the difference between a map of moving dots and a system you can run a business on.
