General
LiftMe is scheduling, live-tracking and billing software built specifically for South African scholar transport operators: the drivers and small businesses who run the daily school run. Each operator gets their own branded subdomain rather than sharing a generic app.
Generic fleet tools track vehicles and jobs. LiftMe is built around learners, parents, drivers and schools specifically: recurring weekly routes, a parent portal per child, and pricing tiers per school, instead of screens meant for courier or logistics dispatch.
Use the Find my company search on the homepage to look up your operator by name, or email us if you've lost the link and we'll track it down.
Pricing & billing
Three plans, billed month to month: Bakkie (R499/mo, up to 2 vehicles), Fleet (R1,299/mo, up to 8 vehicles), and Depot (R2,799/mo, unlimited vehicles). See the full pricing and comparison table.
Yes. If none of the three plans fits, build your own plan: enter your vehicles, children and schools, switch on only the features you want, and see your price and email it to us. It also covers extras that aren't listed on the plans, such as Client risk, Driver transfers, Driver navigation and Off Season.
Billing is month to month. Before you go live we ask you to sign a short agreement covering liability and POPIA compliance, so the responsibilities on both sides are clear.
Fleet and Depot include invoicing generated straight from completed trips, with distance-based pricing tiers per school. Depot adds full per-school tiering and VAT-ready invoices for multi-branch operators.
Yes. Our free school transport invoice template builds an invoice in four quick steps, with learner names, prices and optional VAT, and downloads it as a PDF. It runs in your browser with no sign-up, and nothing you type is uploaded.
Setup & onboarding
Most operators are live within days, not weeks. We set up your subdomain, routes and branding for you.
You get an address like yourcompany.liftme.co.za themed with your logo, colours and fonts. Parents see your business. LiftMe is the software running underneath, not a co-branded product.
No installation. LiftMe runs in the browser for the office and parents, and drivers use a mobile-friendly web app to log pickups and drop-offs from the road.
Yes, but there is nothing to download. LiftMe runs in the browser on any phone, and parents and drivers can add it to their home screen so it opens like any other app, with your company's name and icon.
Yes. Every operator gets their own branded web address with a landing page for the business, a get-a-quote calculator and a parent login, so you do not need to build or host a separate website.
Drivers & parents
Yes, on the Fleet and Depot plans. Live GPS tracking lets parents watch the vehicle from the moment it sets off, alongside their child's trip status.
Drivers work through today's route stop by stop and log pickups, drop-offs and exceptions from their phone as they happen, and the office and parents see the update immediately.
Yes. Schedule changes, delays and confirmations go out to parents and drivers automatically, so the office doesn't have to phone anyone.
Data & security
Only that child's own parent, the assigned driver, and your office admins on your subdomain. Trip and tracking data isn't shared across operators or made public.
Yes. Every operator runs on their own branded subdomain with their own routes, drivers, learners and billing, so one operator can't see another's data.
