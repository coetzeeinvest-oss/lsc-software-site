General
LiftMe is scheduling, live-tracking and billing software built specifically for South African scholar transport operators — the drivers and small businesses who run the daily school run. Each operator gets their own branded subdomain rather than sharing a generic app.
Generic fleet tools track vehicles and jobs. LiftMe is built around learners, parents, drivers and schools specifically — recurring weekly routes, a parent portal per child, and pricing tiers per school, instead of screens meant for courier or logistics dispatch.
Use the Find my company search on the homepage to look up your operator by name, or email us if you've lost the link — we'll track it down.
Pricing & billing
Three plans, billed month to month: Bakkie (R499/mo, up to 2 vehicles), Fleet (R1,299/mo, up to 8 vehicles), and Depot (R2,799/mo, unlimited vehicles). See the full pricing and comparison table.
No annual contract on any plan. Billing is month to month and you can cancel any time.
Fleet and Depot include invoicing generated straight from completed trips, with distance-based pricing tiers per school. Depot adds full per-school tiering and VAT-ready invoices for multi-branch operators.
Yes — your first month is used to set up routes, branding and driver accounts, and isn't billed. Regular billing starts from month two.
Setup & onboarding
Most operators are live within days, not weeks. We set up your subdomain, routes and branding during your free first month.
You get an address like yourcompany.liftme.co.za themed with your logo, colours and fonts. Parents see your business — LiftMe is the software running underneath, not a co-branded product.
No installation — LiftMe runs in the browser for the office and parents, and drivers use a mobile-friendly web app to log pickups and drop-offs from the road.
Drivers & parents
Yes, on the Fleet and Depot plans. Live GPS tracking lets parents watch the vehicle from the moment it sets off, alongside their child's trip status.
Drivers work through today's route stop by stop and log pickups, drop-offs and exceptions from their phone as they happen — the office and parents see the update immediately.
Yes — schedule changes, delays and confirmations go out to parents and drivers automatically, so the office doesn't have to phone anyone.
Data & security
Only that child's own parent, the assigned driver, and your office admins on your subdomain — trip and tracking data isn't shared across operators or made public.
Yes. Every operator runs on their own branded subdomain with their own routes, drivers, learners and billing — one operator can't see another's data.
